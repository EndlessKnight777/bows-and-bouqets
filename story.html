About Our Shop

Nurturing Success

The Journey of Bows & Bouquets

So, it all started with my younger sister. She kicked off this cool gift store right by the hospital, filling it with flowers, snacks, chocolates, and teddies, basically all things happy. When Covid hit, she packed up and headed to Port Shepstone. That is when I decided to jump in and take over the store.

It was a bit like giving the place a facelift, bringing back the good vibes. Now, it has become my mission to keep the joy alive and make the store a cozy spot for everyone around the hospital.

Nestled in a hospital setting

Just outside the hospital hustle, our gift shop is a cozy spot filled with cheer and comfort. Whether you are planning ahead online or spontaneously dropping by, we have got you covered. Our shop is like a warm hug in a bustling hospital environment.

A Note From Naazia

The Heart Behind Bows & Bouquets

Bows & Bouquets by Naazia was never created to be just another flower shop. It was born from Naazia's heart, from her love for beauty, her eye for detail, her softness, her strength, and her belief that the right flowers can say what words sometimes cannot.

To Naazia, flowers are not simply petals and stems. They are emotion. They are memory. They are love made visible. They are the apology someone struggled to say. The celebration someone waited years for. The comfort sent when life feels heavy. The "I thought of you" that arrives exactly when it is needed most.

Every bouquet is created with intention. Every ribbon is chosen with care. Every colour, wrap, message card and finishing touch matters, because Naazia believes people should not just receive flowers, they should feel seen by them.

Behind the brand is a woman who has poured her life, prayers, creativity, tired eyes, wild heart and endless love into building something meaningful. A place where flowers are not rushed together, but thoughtfully created to carry feeling.

"Bows & Bouquets by Naazia is more than flowers and gifts. It is a piece of Naazia herself, wrapped in ribbon, arranged with love, and sent out into the world to make someone's heart feel softer."

This is where beauty meets emotion. Where flowers become language. Where every order is treated with care, because every order belongs to someone's story.
