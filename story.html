About Our Shop

How It Started

How Bows & Bouquets Came To Be

The shop was first opened by my younger sister, right next to the hospital, stocked with flowers, snacks, chocolates and teddies, all the things that put a smile on someone's face. When she relocated to Port Shepstone during the Covid years, I stepped in to keep it running.

It took a bit of rebuilding to get the shop feeling like itself again. These days, keeping that spirit going is what I care about most, and making sure the shop stays a welcoming stop for everyone passing through this part of town.

Right next to the hospital

Just outside the hospital grounds, our little shop is a place people duck into for a bit of comfort. Order ahead online, or pop in on the spot, either way works for us. It is meant to feel like a small breather in the middle of a hectic day.

A Note From Naazia

Why I Do This

This was never meant to be just another flower shop. It came from a genuine love of putting things together by hand, an eye for small details, and a belief that flowers can carry what words sometimes struggle to.

Flowers hold more than they look like they do. A bouquet can be an apology that was hard to put into words, a celebration someone had waited on, or a small reminder that someone is thinking of you on a heavy day.

Nothing here is thrown together. The colours, the wrap, the ribbon, the little card, each choice is made on purpose, because a gift should feel like it was made for the person receiving it, not pulled off a shelf.

This shop is a lot of early mornings, a lot of care and a genuine want to build something that means something to the people who walk in.

"Every bouquet that leaves this shop is tied by hand and meant for someone specific. That part never changes, no matter how busy things get."

Every order gets the same amount of care, whether it is a small thank you or a full event. That is the whole point of doing it this way.
